Monitor 3D

Four 3D prototypes of the Monitor TUI. All of them replay the same 90 recorded mactop samples. Serve this folder's parent over HTTP, or open the files directly.

VT in the room

The real draft-03 TUI on a bendable terminal slab, with 3D core columns and a die anchored to cells, Ratty-style.

Silicon floor

A schematic M2 Pro floorplan whose E, P and GPU cores extrude with load and glow with temperature.

 C

History terrain

Per-core load over the last 90 seconds as a landscape you can orbit and scrub.

Terminal-native 3D

Runs in your terminal. Braille 3D core bars, and real 3D objects when it's running inside Ratty.
